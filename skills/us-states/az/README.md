# Arizona (US-AZ): tax pack

Arizona runs a flat **2.5%** individual income tax (AZDOR Individual Income Tax Highlights, read 2026-10-28: "2025 New Tax Rate of 2.5% for All Income Levels and Filing Status") and a flat **4.9%** corporate income tax with a **$50 minimum** (A.R.S. §43-1111, read 2026-10-28; 2024 Form 120: "Tax is 4.9 percent of line 15 or fifty dollars ($50), whichever is greater"; 2026 Booklet 120/165ES computes estimated tax by 4.9%). There is no state franchise or gross-receipts tax on top of income tax; the sales-tax equivalent is the **Transaction Privilege Tax (TPT)**, a tax on the seller's privilege (AZDOR TPT page, read 2026-10-28), with state classification rates set by A.R.S. §42-5010 and a $100,000 remote-seller economic-nexus threshold (A.R.S. §42-5044, read 2026-10-28). Corporate multistate income is apportioned under a Uniform Division of Income Tax Act structure (§§43-1131 to 43-1149) with property, payroll and sales factors (§§43-1141/43-1143/43-1145 read 2026-10-28); unitary groups file combined and affiliated groups may consolidate (§43-947, cited in §43-1132(D)). Employer social contributions centre on Arizona unemployment insurance (wage base $8,000 per employee per calendar year, A.R.S. §23-622, read 2026-10-28) and rates per the statutory reserve-ratio schedule (§23-730, read 2026-10-28). Corporate returns are due the **15th day of the 4th month** after year-end (AZDOR corporate page, read 2026-10-28). Interest on under/overpayments tracks IRC §6621 (federal short-term + three percentage points), compounded annually (AZDOR interest-rate page, read 2026-10-28).

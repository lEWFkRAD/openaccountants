# Georgia Individual Tax Return (Form 500)

## Georgia Individual Tax Return (Form 500)

Georgia Individual Income Tax Return (Form 500) — STARTER DRAFT
STARTER DRAFT, generic. Your corrections below will make it yours.

Form 500 is Georgia's individual income tax return, filed annually by residents (and, on related variants, part-year residents and nonresidents with Georgia-source income) to report state income tax on the prior calendar or fiscal year. It generally starts from the federal return and applies Georgia-specific adjustments, deductions and credits.

### Who this is for

Full-year Georgia residents with income above the state filing requirement (threshold: confirm before filing).
Part-year residents and nonresidents with Georgia-source income (may use a schedule or variant of the form — confirm before filing).
Anyone due a refund of Georgia withholding or estimated payments even if below the filing threshold.

### Before you start, ask the client

Copy of the completed federal return for the same year (Georgia starts from federal AGI — confirm the exact starting line before filing).
Residency facts: full-year, part-year (move dates), or nonresident with GA-source income.
All W-2s and 1099s showing Georgia withholding.
Estimated payments made to Georgia and any prior-year overpayment applied.
Retirement income details and ages (Georgia has a retirement income exclusion — eligibility ages and amounts: confirm before filing).
Items that may trigger Georgia additions/subtractions: municipal bond interest (GA vs. other states), 529 contributions, HSA items, military pay, Social Security treatment — confirm before filing.
Any Georgia credits claimed or purchased (e.g., low-income credit, credits from pass-throughs, film or other transferable credits — confirm before filing).
Prior-year Georgia return and any notices from the state tax authority.

### The method, step by step

Complete and lock the federal return first; Georgia is derivative of it.
Open the current-year Form 500 in your tax software and confirm the software's state module is updated for the filing year.
Enter residency status and dates; if part-year or nonresident, complete the income-allocation schedule (confirm which schedule before filing).
Check that federal AGI carried to the Georgia starting line matches the federal return exactly.
Enter Georgia additions and subtractions (retirement exclusion, out-of-state muni interest, other adjustments — confirm the current list before filing).
Apply the Georgia standard or itemized deduction and exemptions/dependents as the state defines them (amounts: confirm before filing).
Compute tax at the current Georgia rate (rate structure has changed in recent years — confirm before filing).
Enter withholding and estimated payments; reconcile to the W-2/1099 state boxes, not to the client's memory.
Enter credits with required supporting schedules or certificates attached (confirm attachment rules before filing).
Review the balance due or refund against last year's return; explain any large swing before filing.
E-file through your software or the state tax authority's portal (portal name: confirm before filing); confirm acceptance, not just transmission.
If tax is due, arrange payment by the payment deadline (confirm before filing) and document the client's authorization.

### The traps

Georgia's rate structure changed recently (flat-rate transition); software or memory of old brackets can miscompute — confirm the current rate.
Retirement income exclusion is age- and type-dependent and applies per taxpayer; easy to miss or double-claim for married couples — confirm rules.
Part-year/nonresident allocation: Georgia taxes on a ratio method that differs from some states — confirm the computation.
Federal/state itemizing interaction: whether the client may itemize for Georgia when taking the federal standard deduction — confirm.
Withholding on the W-2 coded to the wrong state for remote workers or recent movers.
Purchased/transferred credits (e.g., film credits) have their own documentation and timing rules — confirm before claiming.
An extension of time to file is not an extension of time to pay — confirm both deadlines.

## Who this applies to

Georgia resident and part-year individual filers (Form 500). Nonresidents and nonresident part-year filers use Form IT-Z390 via ga-cross-border-tax.md.

## Rates, bands and thresholds

Georgia flat individual income tax rate 5.19% for 2025, moving to a flat rate under HB 463 for 2026 (check ga-income-tax.md for the current-year table). Dependent exemption $5,000 for 2026.

## The taxable base

Georgia taxable income starts from federal AGI with Georgia additions and subtractions (Form 500 Schedule 3 and the IT-511 subtraction list). Full detail in ga-income-tax.md.

## Deadlines and filing

Form 500 is due April 15. A federal extension gives six months automatically for the return, but estimated payments and any balance due still follow the original deadline.

## Penalties and interest

Late filing 5% a month up to 25% (O.C.G.A. § 48-7-131); interest at the DOR published rate; underpayment penalty 9% a year (Form 500 UET). See ga-estimated-tax-depth.md.

## Cross-border points

Nonresident Georgia-source income and part-year proration live in ga-cross-border-tax.md; NYC and other city returns are separate.

Filing status, residency through the year, county of residence (for the sales tax return), and any military retirement or unborn-child credits.

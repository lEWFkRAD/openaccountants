# Jamaica tax pack (JM)

> Source-cited draft for tax year 2026. Last source check: 2026-10-24.

## Coverage

### applicable

- `jm-tax-overview.md` — system overview and administration.
- `jm-corporate-income-tax.md` — corporate income tax.
- `jm-gst-gct.md` — General Consumption Tax; this covers the VAT/turnover-tax domain.
- `jm-payroll-withholding.md` — PAYE withholding entry point.
- `jm-payroll-social.md` — PAYE, NIS, NHT, Education Tax and HEART/NSTA payroll contributions.
- `jm-transfer-pricing.md` — arm's-length scope, JMD 500 million gross-revenue threshold, methods and documentation.
- `jm-cross-border-tax.md` — nonresident withholding, treaty and permanent-establishment controls.

### not_applicable

- Estate/inheritance tax — outside issue #298's missing-domain scope; stamp/transfer duties and succession rules require a separate transaction-specific review.
- Separate exit/departure tax — no generally applicable stand-alone corporate exit-tax regime was established from the official sources reviewed for this pack; disposals and migrations can still trigger ordinary tax, transfer-pricing and duty consequences.

## Source status

The Jamaica transfer-pricing and payroll guides use Tax Administration Jamaica (TAJ) material and official Laws of Jamaica records from the Laws of Jamaica information portal. Current treaty entitlement, beneficial ownership, tax-clearance and filing-form mechanics must be confirmed for each payment or filing.

# Dominican Republic tax pack — domain applicability

Jurisdiction: DO (Dominican Republic). As of 2026-10-22. The tax system sits
on the Código Tributario (Law 11-92) and the income-tax law (Law 173-07, as
amended by Law 253-12); the authority is the Dirección General de Impuestos
Internos (DGII). Consumption tax is the ITIS (16%) with a separate selective
consumption tax; social contributions and payroll withholding run through the
TSS (contributive regime) with employer/employee/health rates. Transfer
pricing documentation sits in IT-16-06 (DGII); treaty relief runs through
DO's DTA network (see do-tax-treaties).

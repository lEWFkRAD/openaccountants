# Bahamas: tax pack

The Bahamas imposes no general income or corporate income tax: companies
pay an annual turnover-based business licence tax, VAT, customs duties,
stamp duty and real property tax. Since 1 January 2024 a 15% Domestic
Minimum Top-Up Tax (GloBE/Pillar Two, EUR 750m threshold) applies to
Bahamian entities of in-scope MNE groups, administered by the Financial
Secretary with DIR service delivery — that is where transfer-pricing-style
arm's-length discipline now lives. There is no withholding tax and no
classic DTA network; information exchange runs on TIEAs, FATCA and CRS,
and substance reporting runs under CESRA. Currency BSD is pegged 1:1 to
USD.

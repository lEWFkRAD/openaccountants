# Peru: tax pack

Peru taxes domiciled taxpayers on worldwide income and
non-domiciled persons/entities on Peruvian-source income only
(reinforced use-economically-in-Peru source rules, Income Tax Law
Arts. 6–10). General corporate rate 29.5%; non-resident rates 5% on
dividends and real-estate gains, 5% interest (30% when linked or via
low-tax jurisdictions), and a deemed branch-profit distribution at the
annual-return date. The consumption tax is the 16% IGV (with a 2%
municipal promotion surcharge on many supplies) administered by SUNAT
under the TUO (D.S. 055-99-EF). Transfer pricing is statutory: Art.
32-A market value, six listed methods, Estudio Técnico documentation.
Currency sol (S/); calendar tax year. Primary reads of 2026-10-23 are
the SUNAT chapter PDFs (legislacion/renta/ley/cap*.pdf and
legislacion/codigo/libro*/libro.pdf); the DTT treaty list and UIT
thresholds did not resolve and are flagged needs-sources.

# Dominica: tax pack

Dominica taxes income under the Income Tax Act, Chapter 67:01 of the Laws
of the Commonwealth of Dominica, administered by the Inland Revenue
Division (IRD) within the Ministry of Finance. The tax year for individuals
is the calendar year (returns due 31 March); companies file within three
months after financial year-end and pay three quarterly instalments at
25%/35%/40%. The ordinary company rate is 25% on net profit per the IRD's
current rate schedule. Value-added tax (16%) replaced the old sales tax
under the Value Added Tax Act; social security runs through the Dominica
Social Security Organisation (SSO), separate from the IRD. Withholding on
payments to non-residents is 15% under ITA ss. 53–57 and the Third
Schedule, remitted within 15 days after month-end. Dominica's treaty
network is essentially the CARICOM Double Taxation Agreement Order 2008;
the IRD maintains Country-by-Country Reporting and MAP landing pages under
its international-tax section.

**Source status:** ird.gov.dm pages (personal income tax, corporate income
tax, withholding tax, current rates, international-tax section) resolve and
were read 2026-10-24. The statute PDF at dominica.gov.dm
(chap67-01.pdf) is a **scanned image with no text layer** — article-level
quotes are **needs-sources**; the transfer-pricing domain in particular
rests on the Act's associated-persons provisions whose text could not be

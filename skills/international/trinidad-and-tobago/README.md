# Trinidad and Tobago: tax pack

Trinidad and Tobago taxes individuals and companies under the Income
Tax Act (Chap. 75:01) and the Corporation Tax Act (Chap. 75:02),
administered by the Inland Revenue Division (IRD) of the Ministry of
Finance; social insurance runs through the National Insurance Board
(NIBTT) under the National Insurance Act (Chap. 32:01). VAT is 15%
under the VAT Act (Chap. 75:06). The currency is the Trinidad and
Tobago dollar (TTD); the year of income is the calendar year, with
corporation tax returns due 30 April and quarterly instalments. TT
levies two distinctive company surcharges — the 0.6% Business Levy and
the 0.3% Green Fund Levy. The treaty network is real and listed by the
IRD (fifteen instruments, from the USA 1971 to Spain 2009), and the IRD
publishes Mutual Agreement Procedure guidelines; transfer-pricing
adjustment runs through the Income Tax Act's associated-person
provisions rather than a standalone TP code.

**Source status:** ird.gov.tt, nibtt.net, finance.gov.tt and
laws.gov.tt all resolved on 2026-10-24; the consolidated statute texts
in the Digital Legislative Library were not machine-readable this pass,

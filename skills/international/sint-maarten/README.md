# Sint Maarten: tax pack

Sint Maarten (SX, Dutch side of Saint Martin) runs a small open tax system administered by the Tax Administration Sint Maarten (GRC — Belastingdienst), Vineyard Building, Philipsburg (tax.sx, Taxinfo@sintmaartengov.org). Currency is the Netherlands Antillean guilder (Cg/NAf), pegged 1 USD = 1.80 Cg. Individuals: progressive schedule 12.5%–47.5% with a nil threshold of Cg 27,281.28 for 2026, set annually by Ministerial regulation (AB 2026 no. 11) under the income-tax and wage-tax ordinances. Employers withhold wage tax monthly on GRC tables. Consumption tax is a 5% turnover tax (ontvangstenmodel, receipts-based) under Landsverordening CVDR697488 — not a VAT. Companies pay profit tax (winstbelasting) with a provisional return due 31 March and final return due 30 June. Social insurance: AOV/AWW premiums 14% total (employer 7.5% / employee 6.5%) plus AVBZ 2% (0.5%/1.5%) per the GRC's official rate illustration; statutory caps are year-specific. There is no domestic transfer-pricing documentation regime found in the official sources; cross-border work runs through the non-resident "B-return" and the Kingdom of the Netherlands framework.

**Source status:** official GRC tables (AB 2026 no. 11 + Annexes I/II), the turnover-tax ordinance (lokaleregelgeving CVDR697488), the general tax-ordinance (CVDR671742) and GRC forms/guides resolve and were read 2026-10-27. The profit-tax rate itself is NOT printed on the official return forms and the profit-tax ordinance text was not retrievable that day — the IT-CORP skill carries a needs-sources caveat on the rate. AOV/AWW/AVBZ caps are 2020 figures from the official GRC guide; 2026 caps need confirmation.

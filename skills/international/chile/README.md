# Chile: tax pack

Chile runs a partially integrated corporate/personal system. First
Category Tax (FCT, Art. 11 LIR) is 27% for entities in the Partially
Integrated System (PIS) and 25% for pro-Pyme SMEs, on worldwide income
of resident entities. Owners then pay the Global Complementary Tax
(IGC, progressive to 35.5% on USD-equivalent UTM bands) or, for
non-residents, a 35% 'additional' withholding on distributions with
the FCT partially (65%) creditable in PIS — a 44.45% combined ceiling
for non-treaty dividend chains (PwC Chile, read 2026-10-24). Payroll
withholding covers second-category tax on wages, the AFP 10% pension
and 7% health deductions, and the boleta de honorarios withholding,
which is **15.25% for 2026** (SII, read 2026-10-24; stepping to 17%
by 2028). Transfer pricing follows the OECD-aligned Art. 41 bis LIR
(Ley 21.210/2020) with 3:1 thin capitalisation. Administration is the
Servicio de Impuestos Internos (SII, sii.cl); annual returns are due
before 30 April; the statute of limitations is 3 years (6 if no return
or a maliciously false return). Currency: peso (CLP); UF/UTM units
index the brackets.

**Source status:** sii.cl resolved on 2026-10-24 (boleta de honorarios
2026 withholding page confirms 15.25%; SII TP sub-pages did not
resolve — Art. 41 bis text is **needs-sources** via leychile.cl,
which serves a JS shell). PwC Chile chapters (withholding reviewed
19 December 2025) supply the treaty table and the 44.45% formula.
UTM/UF bracket values for 2026 are **needs-sources** — the IGC/second-
category tables below carry PwC's USD-equivalent bands with their
2017 base-date caveat.

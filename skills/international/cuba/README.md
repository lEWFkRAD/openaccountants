# Cuba: tax pack

Cuba's system runs on Law No. 113 of 2012 (Ley del Sistema Tributario),
amended by the 2021 Tax Codes (Decree-Law 34) and every year by the State
Budget Law. ONAT administers; currency is the Cuban peso (CUP); the tax
year is the calendar year. There is no VAT — a sales tax and a services
tax stand in its place (the `cu-vat-gst` skill covers that consumption
layer). Corporate profits take the Impuesto sobre Utilidades at 35%
general, 15% for Ley 118 foreign-investment entities, up to 50% for
natural resources. There is no standalone transfer-pricing code and the
treaty network is not published at a stable official path — both new
skills carry explicit source-status caveats.

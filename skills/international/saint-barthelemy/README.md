# Saint-Barthélemy: tax pack

Saint-Barthélemy has fiscal autonomy and a territorial Contributions Code. It uses the euro and imposes specific levies, including the annual business contribution, tourist-stay tax, and immovable-property gains tax, rather than a general local personal or corporate income tax or VAT. The five-year fiscal-domicile rule and property-gain rules make cross-border status especially important.

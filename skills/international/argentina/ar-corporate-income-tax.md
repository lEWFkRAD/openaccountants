# Argentina Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate income tax rates and base (2026)

- **Corporate rate — tier 1** — 25% on taxable income from ARS 0 up to ARS 5,000,000 (base scale amounts; fixed in Ley 27.630 art. 3, indexed annually — see indexation note)  _(Ley 20.628 t.o. 2019, art. 73 a), as amended by Ley 27.630 — archived: SRC-AR-ley-27630-arca-biblioteca.html, SRC-AR-decreto-824-2019-ordenado.html)_
- **Corporate rate — tier 2** — ARS 1,250,000 + 30% on the amount exceeding ARS 5,000,000 up to ARS 50,000,000 (base scale amounts)  _(Ley 20.628 t.o. 2019, art. 73 a), Ley 27.630 art. 3 — archived: SRC-AR-ley-27630-arca-biblioteca.html)_
- **Corporate rate — tier 3 (top)** — ARS 14,750,000 + 35% on taxable income exceeding ARS 50,000,000 (base scale amounts)  _(Ley 20.628 t.o. 2019, art. 73 a), Ley 27.630 art. 3 — archived: SRC-AR-ley-27630-arca-biblioteca.html)_
- **Corporate bracket indexation** — The 25/30/35% scale amounts are adjusted annually from 1 January 2022 by the October-over-October IPC (CPI) published by INDEC; the adjusted amounts apply to fiscal years beginning after each update. The ARS 5M/50M figures above are the statutory base amounts — use the ARCA-published updated scale for the current fiscal year  _(Ley 20.628 t.o. 2019, art. 73, final paragraph (incorporated by Ley 27.630 art. 5) — archived: SRC-AR-decreto-824-2019-ordenado.html)_
- **Permanent establishments (branch tax)** — Foreign-company PEs (art. 22) pay the same 25/30/35% scale on Argentine-source income, plus an additional 7% tax payable at the time profits are remitted to the head office  _(Ley 20.628 t.o. 2019, art. 73 b), as substituted by Ley 27.630 art. 4 — archived: SRC-AR-decreto-824-2019-ordenado.html)_
- **Casino / gambling special rate** — Income from casino games (roulette, punto y banca, blackjack, poker, authorized games), electronic gambling machines, automated betting and digital betting platforms is taxed at 41.50% (applies to individuals and companies alike)  _(Ley 20.628 t.o. 2019, art. 73 b), second paragraph — archived: SRC-AR-decreto-824-2019-ordenado.html)_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide net taxable income of resident companies, with inflation adjustment (ajuste por inflación impositivo) where applicable  _(Ley 20.628 t.o. 2019 — archived: SRC-AR-ley-20628-ordenado.html)_
- **Dividend / profit-distribution withholding** — When dividends or profit distributions (cash or in kind) exceed accumulated taxed profits determined under general rules at the close of the prior fiscal year, the payer must withhold 35% on the excess, as a final (single-and-definitive) payment in lieu of the beneficiary's own tax  _(Ley 20.628 t.o. 2019, art. 74 — archived: SRC-AR-decreto-824-2019-ordenado.html)_
- **Withholding tax on interest to non-residents** — 35% applied to a deemed profit margin; effective rate commonly 15.05% (or 35% in some cases), subject to treaty reduction %  _([Ley 20.628, beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties to non-residents** — 35% applied to a deemed margin; effective rates vary by type (e.g. ~31.5% software, ~28% trademark/technical assistance, reducible to ~21% in certain cases), subject to treaty reduction % ((approx — confirm effective rate per royalty type))  _([Ley 20.628, beneficiarios del exterior](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/argentina/corporate/withholding-taxes))_
- **Tax treaties** — Argentina has a network of 20+ double tax treaties that may reduce dividend, interest and royalty withholding rates  _(Double Taxation Agreements (Convenios para evitar la doble imposición))_
- **CIT filing & payment deadline** — Annual return generally due in the fifth/sixth month after fiscal year-end; extended to June of the following year for calendar-year companies under recent resolutions ((approx — confirm exact CUIT-based dates))  _(ARCA General Resolution (e.g. RG 5648/2025))_
- **Advance payments** — Companies make monthly/periodic advance income-tax payments (anticipos) during the year toward the annual liability  _(ARCA General Resolution on income-tax advances (anticipos))_

# Curaçao: tax pack

Curaçao taxes profit and income under the Landssverordening winstbelasting
(Profit Tax Ordinance) and Landswet inkomstenbelasting (Income Tax). The
2023 Corporate Tax Reform (effective 2024) set the two-tier Profit Tax —
22% standard, 15% for small companies (turnover under NAg 5m) — and a
Pillar Two 15% minimum tax for groups of EUR 750m+. The OTB
(Ontvanger Belastingen) administers; currency is the Netherlands Antillean
guilder (NAg/ANG, pegged 1.79/USD); the tax year is the calendar year.
Turnover tax (BAV) was replaced by a VAT-style regime in the reform —
the `cw-vat-gst` skill covers the consumption layer.

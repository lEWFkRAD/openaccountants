# Uruguay: tax pack

Uruguay runs a territorial-source system administered by the Dirección
General Impositiva (DGI): resident entities and Uruguayan permanent
establishments pay IRAE (25%) on Uruguay-source business income,
individuals pay IRPF on labour income (progressive to 36%, employer-
withheld through BPS) and IRNR on Uruguay-source income of
non-residents. IVA (22% standard / 10% reduced) applies to goods and
services; social contributions run through the Banco de Previsión
Social (BPS). The currency is the Uruguayan peso (UYU); the tax year
is the calendar year. A 15% domestic minimum top-up tax (IMC, Ley
20.341) implements Pillar Two for in-scope groups. Uruguay maintains a
25-treaty double-taxation network listed by the DGI with entry-into-
force dates, most modified by the OECD MLI.

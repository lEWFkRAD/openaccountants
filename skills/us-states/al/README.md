# Alabama (US-AL) pack

Jurisdiction: Alabama, United States. Tax year 2026. Pack drafted 2026-10-27 (source-cited drafts, tier 2).

Alabama taxes corporations at a flat 6.5% of net income with a deduction for federal income
tax paid, on single-sales-factor apportionment for tax years beginning on or after 2021-01-01.
The same Office of Revenue administers withholding (A-1/A-2/A-3/A-6) and the sales tax;
unemployment contributions run through the Department of Labor (labor.alabama.gov /
adol.alabama.gov). There is no standalone transfer-pricing statute: related-party economics
are policed through combined reporting and the single-sales-factor apportionment. Individuals
pay a graduated income tax (see `al-income-tax`).

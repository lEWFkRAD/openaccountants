# Curacao Personal Income Tax

## Personal income tax bands and rates (2025)

- **Band 1 rate (up to ANG 38,225)** — 9.75 percent (Taxable income up to ANG 38,225)  _([Ministeriële regeling vaststelling bedragen inkomstenbelasting 2025, P.B. 2024 no. 143, art. 1 — archived PDF](https://belastingdienst.cw/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/143.-MR-vaststelling-bedragen-IB-2025.pdf))_
- **Band 2 rate (ANG 38,225 to 50,968)** — 15.00 percent, plus fixed amount ANG 3,727 (over ANG 38,225 up to ANG 50,968)  _(Ministeriële regeling IB 2025, P.B. 2024 no. 143, art. 1 (archived PDF))_
- **Band 3 rate (ANG 50,968 to 76,452)** — 23.00 percent, plus fixed amount ANG 5,638 (over ANG 50,968 up to ANG 76,452)  _(Ministeriële regeling IB 2025, P.B. 2024 no. 143, art. 1 (archived PDF))_
- **Band 4 rate (ANG 76,452 to 108,306)** — 30.00 percent, plus fixed amount ANG 11,500 (over ANG 76,452 up to ANG 108,306)  _(Ministeriële regeling IB 2025, P.B. 2024 no. 143, art. 1 (archived PDF))_
- **Band 5 rate (ANG 108,306 to 159,273)** — 37.50 percent, plus fixed amount ANG 21,056 (over ANG 108,306 up to ANG 159,273)  _(Ministeriële regeling IB 2025, P.B. 2024 no. 143, art. 1 (archived PDF))_
- **Band 6 rate (over ANG 159,273)** — 46.50 percent, plus fixed amount ANG 40,168 (over ANG 159,273)  _(Ministeriële regeling IB 2025, P.B. 2024 no. 143, art. 1 (archived PDF))_
- **Top marginal personal income tax rate** — 46.5 percent (the Belastingdienst's own tarieven page states "een tarief tussen 9,75% tot en met 46,5%")  _([Belastingdienst Curaçao, Belastingtarieven](https://belastingdienst.cw/particulier/themas/belastingtarieven/))_
- **Basic credit (basiskorting) 2025** — ANG 2,850, with alone-earner supplement (alleenverdienertoeslag) ANG 1,739, elderly supplement (ouderentoeslag) ANG 1,312 (transferable part ANG 658), and child supplements (kindertoeslag) ANG 927 / 464 / 121 / 94 by category  _(Ministeriële regeling IB 2025, P.B. 2024 no. 143, art. 2 (archived PDF))_
- **Residence test** — Residence is determined by facts and circumstances (place of permanent home, centre of vital interests); an individual habitually living in Curacao is a resident  _(General National Ordinance on National Taxes (Algemene landsverordening Landsbelastingen))_
- **Resident scope** — Residents are taxed on worldwide income; non-residents only on Curacao-source income (e.g. local employment, local real estate, local business)  _(Income Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de inkomstenbelasting 1943))_
- **Penshonado (retiree) regime** — Qualifying retirees aged 50+ who become resident after 5+ years abroad may elect a flat 10% tax on foreign-source income (or a deemed-income basis) percent  _([Income Tax Ordinance — penshonado provisions (Landsverordening op de inkomstenbelasting 1943)](https://www.sebsauerborn.com/countries/curacao))_
- **Expatriate (deskundige) regime** — Qualifying foreign experts may exclude certain reimbursements; from 1 February 2025 the regime requires mandatory training of local employees  _([Income Tax Ordinance / Wage Tax expatriate decree](https://www.grantthornton.com.cw/insights/TaxLawChanges2025/))_
- **Wage tax as advance levy** — Wage tax (loonbelasting) withheld by employers is creditable against the final income tax liability  _(Wage Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de loonbelasting 1976))_
- **Income tax return filing deadline** — Within the period stated on the assessment/invitation; commonly by 31 May following the tax year, with extensions available on request (approx — confirm exact date)  _(General National Ordinance on National Taxes (Algemene landsverordening Landsbelastingen))_

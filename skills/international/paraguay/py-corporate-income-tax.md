# Paraguay corporate income tax (IRE)

## 1. Who this applies to

- Paraguayan companies and permanent establishments/branches of foreign companies, on **Paraguay-source** income only — Paraguay's system is territorial (PwC Paraguay, corporate CIT, read 2026-10-24).
- IRE (Impuesto a la Renta Empresarial, Ley 72/2023) applies to income, profits and earnings from primary, secondary and tertiary activities — agricultural, commercial, industrial or services — and to income generated by assets, rights and obligations and acts of disposition of them (PwC, read 2026-10-24).
- **IRE SIMPLE**: optional regime for taxpayers whose income accrued in the previous fiscal year did not exceed **PYG 2,000,000,000**; tax is settled on real or presumed net income, **whichever is less** (PwC, read 2026-10-24).
- **RESIMPLE**: monthly fixed-sum regime for sole proprietorships with previous-year gross income up to **PYG 80,000,000**; monthly amounts run **PYG 20,000–80,000** depending on annual income (PwC, read 2026-10-24).
- Out of scope: IRP on personal services and capital of individuals (`paraguay-income-tax`), IVA (`paraguay-iva`), IPS contributions (`paraguay-social-contributions`).

## 2. Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | Amount | Tax year | Source (as_of 2026-10-24) |
|---|---|---|---|
| IRE general rate | 10% on net Paraguay-source income | 2026 | PwC Paraguay corporate CIT (last reviewed 19 Oct 2026); statutory basis Ley 72/2023 — gazette text needs-sources |
| IRE SIMPLE eligibility | prior-year income ≤ PYG 2,000,000,000 | 2026 | PwC Paraguay corporate CIT |
| IRE SIMPLE basis | min(real net, presumed net) × 10% | 2026 | PwC Paraguay corporate CIT |
| RESIMPLE eligibility | prior-year gross ≤ PYG 80,000,000 | 2026 | PwC Paraguay corporate CIT |
| RESIMPLE monthly tax | PYG 20,000–80,000 by income band | 2026 | PwC Paraguay corporate CIT |
| Local income taxes | none (municipal real-estate tax 1% of fiscal value is separate) | 2026 | PwC Paraguay corporate CIT / other taxes |

## 3. The base

- Taxable base is net income: gross Paraguay-source income less costs and expenses that are necessary to produce and maintain the source of income, plus other deductions allowed by law (PwC, read 2026-10-24).
- **Formal-substantiation risk**: DNIT's stated audit focus is purchase invoices — an expense is deductible only if the supporting vouchers meet the formal requirements established by law (PwC Paraguay tax administration, read 2026-10-24). **AUDIT FLASH POINT** — missing or malformed electronic invoices are the first deduction disallowance.
- Certain expense categories are deductible only up to **1% of gross income** (carried from `paraguay-income-tax`, items 7, 8, 20, 21, 22 of the deductions article and RESIMPLE-sourced purchases; RESEARCH GAP flagged there — confirm against the current statute).
- Loss carry-forward mechanics under IRE: **needs-sources** (statute text not reachable 2026-10-24).

## 4. Deadlines and filing

- Taxable period: **calendar year** (PwC Paraguay tax administration, read 2026-10-24).
- Annual income tax return: self-assessment, filed by the **fourth month after fiscal-year end** (i.e. by 30 April for calendar-year taxpayers) (PwC, read 2026-10-24).
- Payment of annual tax: due on varying days in the fourth month following year end, keyed to the last digits of the taxpayer ID, per the Treasury Ministry calendar (PwC, read 2026-10-24).
- **Advance payments**: four equal instalments calculated on 100% of the prior year's tax, due **May, July, September and November** (Pwc, read 2026-10-24).
- Returns and payments are filed through the DNIT portal (set.gov.py, HTTP 200 on 2026-10-24).

## 5. Penalties and interest

From PwC Paraguay tax administration (read 2026-10-24):

- Late payment of income tax: fine from **4% up to 14%**, plus interest at **0.116% per day**.
- Tax fraud: charge of **one to three times** the tax in default.
- Omission of payment: fine of **50%** of the tax pending.
- Formal infringements: fines equivalent to **USD 10–280**.
- Statute of limitations: the tax authority may audit the **last five fiscal years**.
- Obligatory external tax audit when gross income reaches **PYG 6 billion** in the year.

## 6. Cross-border points

- Territorial basis: foreign-source income is outside the IRE base; only Paraguay-source income is taxed (PwC, read 2026-10-24). Source classification for platform and cross-border payouts is covered in `paraguay-income-tax` §6.5.
- Non-resident companies are taxed on Paraguay-source income earned through activities, assets or rights located in Paraguay; see `py-cross-border-tax`.
- Paraguay's double-tax-treaty network is limited; current treaty entitlement is **needs-sources**.

## 7. Worked examples

**Example 1 — standard IRE.** A Paraguayan S.R.L. earns Paraguay-source net income of PYG 900,000,000 in fiscal 2026. IRE = 900,000,000 × 10% = PYG 90,000,000, filed by 30 April 2027 with advances credited.

**Example 2 — IRE SIMPLE presumed basis (edge case).** A medium-sized company qualifies for IRE SIMPLE (prior-year income PYG 1.5bn). Real net income is PYG 240,000,000 but the presumed net base is PYG 180,000,000. The regime settles on the lesser base: 180,000,000 × 10% = PYG 18,000,000.

```oa-check
name: IRE SIMPLE lesser-of bases
inputs:
  real_net: 240000000
  presumed_net: 180000000
steps:
  - base: min(real_net, presumed_net)
  - tax: base * 0.10
expected:
  tax: 18000000.00
```

## 8. Ask the client first

1. Which regime applies — general IRE, IRE SIMPLE, or RESIMPLE — and what was prior-year income?
2. Is all revenue Paraguay-source? Any foreign customers or foreign-performed services?
3. Do all expense vouchers meet DNIT's formal invoice requirements?
4. Were the four advances (May/Jul/Sep/Nov) paid, and do they credit against the annual return?
5. Is gross income at or above PYG 6bn (obligatory external tax audit)?

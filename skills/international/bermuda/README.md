# Bermuda: tax pack

Bermuda historically had no income or corporate tax; since 1 January 2025 a
15% Corporate Income Tax applies, but only to Bermuda constituent entities
of in-scope MNE groups (EUR 750 million consolidated revenue, two of four
years), computed on GloBE-aligned net income and administered by the
Corporate Income Tax Agency (CITA). Everything else is indirect: payroll
levies (social insurance, employment training levy), VAT, import duty, and
land tax. There is no dividend, interest or royalty withholding. The
transfer-pricing rules sit inside the CIT Act and only bite in-scope
groups; the treaty network is narrow.

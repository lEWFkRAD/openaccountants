# Montserrat: tax pack

Montserrat (British Overseas Territory, Caribbean) levies income and
corporation tax under the Income and Corporation Tax Act (Cap. 17.01,
30% company rate), a single-stage Consumption Tax plus CARICOM Common
External Tariff customs duties at the border under the Customs Duties
and Consumption Tax Act (Cap. 17.05) — there is no VAT — and social
security contributions. The consolidated 1 January 2025 revisions of
Cap. 17.01, Cap. 17.05 and the Tax Administration Act (Cap. 17.26)
resolve from the official legislation portal and were read 2026-10-23.
Currency is the East Caribbean dollar (XCD). There is no transfer
pricing chapter; the arm's-length standard is an anti-avoidance
adjustment power (Cap. 17.26 s.35). No treaty list or CbCR statute
published locally — flagged needs-sources.

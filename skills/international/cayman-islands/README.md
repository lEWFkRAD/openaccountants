# Cayman Islands: tax pack

The Cayman Islands levies no income, corporate, capital-gains or
withholding tax in the ordinary sense: companies pay annual government
fees under the Companies Act, and the Department for International Tax
Cooperation (DITC) administers the international tax-cooperation
frameworks — economic substance, CRS, FATCA, CbCR and exchange of
information — under the Tax Information Authority Act (2021 Revision)
and the International Tax Co-operation (Economic Substance) Act (2026
Revision). A Cayman Income Tax Act was enacted in 2025 to implement the
Pillar Two domestic minimum top-up tax for large multinational groups;
its consolidated text did not resolve from an official page this pass
and is flagged needs-sources. The currency is the Cayman Islands dollar
(CI$); the consumption tax is a 27% import duty schedule rather than a
VAT.

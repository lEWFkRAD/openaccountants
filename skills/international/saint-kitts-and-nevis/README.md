# Saint Kitts and Nevis: tax pack

Saint Kitts and Nevis taxes income and consumption under its own revised statutes, in East Caribbean dollars (XCD, pegged USD 2.70). The Income Tax Act (Cap. 18.22) carries the progressive personal schedule (to 55%), the 33% company rate (38% banks/insurance), quarterly instalments, PAYE and the associated-persons rules. The Value Added Tax Act (Cap. 18.47) carries the 17% standard rate on monthly periods. There is no capital gains tax, no wealth tax and no estate duty, and no comprehensive DTA network — only unilateral relief under the Double Taxation (Relief) Act.

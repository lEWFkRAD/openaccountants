# Saint Vincent and the Grenadines: tax pack

SVG taxes on a mostly residence basis administered by the Inland Revenue Department (IRD, etax.gov.vc). Tax year for individuals is the calendar year; companies file by financial year. Currency is the Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD/EC$). Personal income tax uses a standard deduction plus graduated Fifth Schedule bands; there is a 16% VAT with an 11% accommodation rate; companies pay 28% corporate income tax (Finance Act 10 of 2023, corroborated by the IRD tax page — the Finance Act PDF on finance.gov.vc returned 503 on 2026-10-27). There is no self-assessed transfer-pricing documentation regime; arm's-length adjustments are Comptroller-driven (Income Tax Act Cap. 435).

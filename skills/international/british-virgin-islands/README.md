# British Virgin Islands: tax pack

The BVI is a no-direct-tax jurisdiction: no personal or corporate
income tax, no capital gains tax, no withholding tax, and no VAT/GST.
The direct levies on activity are Payroll Tax (10% Class 1 / 14%
Class 2 of remuneration, first US$10,000 per employee exempt), Social
Security (8.5% total) and National Health Insurance (7.5% total),
administered by the Inland Revenue Department and the statutory
boards; companies pay fixed annual government fees under the BVI
Business Companies Act and file annual financial returns and
economic substance reports through their registered agent. The
currency is the US dollar. Because there is no income tax, the
cross-border and transfer-pricing questions are about **other**
countries' charges on BVI positions: outbound withholding in the
counterparty state, economic substance enforcement, information
exchange (CRS/FATCA), and the OECD Pillar Two 15% top-up for
in-scope groups.

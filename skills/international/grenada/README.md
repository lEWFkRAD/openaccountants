# Grenada: tax pack

Grenada taxes under the Income Tax Act Cap. 149 (consolidated to Act No. 2 of 2010), administered by the Inland Revenue Division (IRD), a division of the Ministry of Finance, now operating the G-TAX online portal (ird.gd/gtax, tax.gov.gd). Tax year for individuals is the calendar year; companies file by basis period with monthly one-twelfth instalments. Currency is the Eastern Caribbean dollar (XCD/EC$). The consolidated statute's Fifth Schedule sets a flat 30% on both individual and company chargeable income; withholding on payments to non-residents is 15% and final (s.50 + Third Schedule). VAT and social contributions are covered by the existing `gd-vat-registration` and `gd-social-contributions` skills; Annual Stamp Tax by `gd-annual-stamp-tax`.

**Source status:** the consolidated Cap. 149 PDF consolidates amendments only to Act No. 2 of 2010; 2011–2026 amendment acts were not retrievable in consolidated form on 2026-10-27, and the main www.ird.gd tax pages returned HTTP 500 that day. Skills carry needs-sources caveats where a figure could have moved by amendment.

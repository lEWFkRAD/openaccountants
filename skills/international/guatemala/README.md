# Guatemala: tax pack

Guatemala taxes income under the Ley de Actualizacion Tributaria (Decreto
10-2012) on a Guatemalan-source basis: the general corporate regime is 25%
on net profit with an optional simplified gross regime at 5%/7%; individuals
pay progressive employment brackets. The consumption tax is IVA (Impuesto
Valor Agregado) at a flat 12% under Decreto 27-92 as amended, administered
by SAT (Superintendencia de Administracion Tributaria) with monthly returns
due by the 15th working day of the following month. The currency is the
quetzal (GTQ); the income tax year is the calendar year. Transfer pricing
runs through Libro I, Chapter VI of Decreto 10-2012 (normas especiales de
valoracion entre partes relacionadas) and the related-party annex to the
annual ISR return. Guatemala's income-tax treaty network is very narrow —
only a handful of double-taxation conventions are in force.

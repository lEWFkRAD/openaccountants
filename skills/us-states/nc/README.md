# North Carolina (US-NC): tax pack

North Carolina levies a flat individual income tax -- 3.99% for taxable years beginning after 2025, with a statutory trigger that can cut it to as low as 2.49% (G.S. 105-153.7) -- and a corporate income tax on a published phase-out schedule: 2.25% (2025), 2% (2026–2027), 1% (2028), 0% after 2029 (G.S. 105-130.3), alongside a franchise tax of $1.50 per $1,000 of the tax base (G.S. 105-122). State sales tax plus local options are administered by the NCDOR; unemployment insurance (the only state-level social contribution, employer-only) is administered by the Division of Employment Security (DES), not the NCDOR. There is no state-level employee social contribution and no state disability or paid-family-leave payroll tax.

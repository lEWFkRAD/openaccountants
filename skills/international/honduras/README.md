# Honduras tax pack (HN)

> Source-cited draft for tax year 2026. Last source check: 2026-10-24.

## Coverage

### applicable

- `hn-tax-overview.md` — system overview and administration.
- `hn-corporate-income-tax.md` — corporate income tax.
- `hn-vat-isv.md` — Impuesto Sobre Ventas (ISV); this is the VAT/turnover-tax domain, so no duplicate VAT skill was created.
- `hn-payroll-withholding.md` and `honduras-payroll.md` — payroll and wage withholding.
- `honduras-social-contributions.md` — IHSS/RAP/INFOP and related employer obligations.
- `hn-transfer-pricing.md` — arm's-length rules, documentation, return and penalties.
- `hn-cross-border-tax.md` — nonresident withholding and outbound-payment controls.

### not_applicable

- Estate/inheritance tax — outside the missing-domain scope of issue #296; obtain a separate succession-law review before advising on transfers at death.
- Exit/departure tax — no separate generally applicable corporate exit-tax regime was established from the official sources reviewed for this pack; asset transfers and restructurings can still trigger ordinary income tax and transfer-pricing rules.

## Source status

The transfer-pricing and nonresident-income rules are anchored to SAR-hosted consolidated legislation and the official-gazette text of Agreement 027-2015. The existing ISV and social-contribution guides already cover those domains and were not duplicated. Treaty entitlement, current SAR form mechanics, and any post-publication administrative changes must be checked at the filing date.

## Primary authorities

- Servicio de Administración de Rentas (SAR): <https://www.sar.gob.hn/>
- SAR legal library: <https://www.sar.gob.hn/download-category/otras-leyes/>

- Income Tax Law, consolidated by SAR: <https://www.sar.gob.hn/download/consolidado-ley-impuesto-sobre-la-renta-25-junio-2018/>

# Suriname: tax pack

Suriname taxes individuals and companies under the Income Tax Act 1922
(Inkomstenbelasting 1922) and the Wage Tax Act (Loonbelasting),
administered by the tax service of the Ministry of Finance and Planning
(Ministerie van Financiën en Planning); there is no independent revenue
authority website that resolves from outside the country. VAT (BTW, Wet
BTW 2022) replaced the turnover tax on 1 January 2023: 10% on goods, 8%
on services, with reduced and luxury bands. The currency is the
Surinamese dollar (SRD); the tax year is the calendar year. Suriname's
income-tax treaty network is very narrow (a Netherlands treaty and a
Curaçao arrangement), and there is no published standalone
transfer-pricing code — related-party pricing runs through the general
income-tax anti-avoidance provisions.

**Source status:** as of 2026-10-24 only gov.sr (and the ministry page
under it) resolves from this drafting environment; the tax
administration, central bank, and statute-portal domains
(belastingkantoor.sr, scb.org.sr, surinamewetten.sr) do not resolve at
all. Statute-level section citations throughout this pack are therefore
**needs-sources** and figures carry the citations of the earlier
source-cited drafts until a local accountant confirms them.

# Ecuador: tax pack

Ecuador taxes corporate income at 25% general (higher with tax-haven
shareholders) under the Ley de Régimen Tributario Interno (LRTI);
individuals pay progressive income tax; IVA is the consumption tax. The
SRI (Servicio de Rentas Internas) administers; the currency is the US
dollar (Ecuador is dollarized); the tax year is the calendar year.
Outbound flows carry 25% withholding on interest and royalties and 25% on
40% of dividends (effective ~10%), with Andean Community Decision 578 and
treaty relief where applicable. Transfer pricing is enforced through the
LRTI arm's-length obligation and the SRI's cooperative-compliance
programme.

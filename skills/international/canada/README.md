# Canada tax pack — domain applicability

Jurisdiction: CA (Canada, federal; provinces and territories carry their own
income-tax and sales-tax layers, several of which have their own sub-packs in
this folder). As of 2026-10-22. Canada taxes individuals and companies
federally under the Income Tax Act (R.S.C., 1985, c. 1 (5th Supp.)) with
parallel provincial statutes; consumption tax is the GST (Excise Tax Act,
R.S.C., 1985, c. 45 (5th Supp.)) with HST/PST/QST overlays. Social
contributions are CPP/EI; payroll withholding is administered by the CRA.
Transfer pricing sits in ITA s. 247 (plus s. 247.1 CbCR); cross-border rules
cover departure tax (s. 128.1), non-resident capital gains (s. 116) and the
treaty network.

# Georgia (US-GA): tax pack

Georgia levies a flat individual income tax (5.19% for 2025 per HB 111, reduced to 4.99% "for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026" per HB 463 (2026)), a corporate income tax at the same flat rate -- HB 1023 (2024) requires corporations to pay "at the same rate of the tax imposed on individuals" -- a separate graduated net worth tax (capped at $5,000), state sales tax (4% state rate plus local options), and employer withholding plus Georgia Unemployment Insurance administered by the Georgia Department of Labor (GDOL) -- a different agency from the Department of Revenue (DOR). There is no state-level social-contribution regime for employees.

**Source status:** DOR booklets (IT-511, IT-611), the HB 111, HB 463 and HB 1199 texts, the DOR withholding page and the 2026 Employer's Tax Guide are archived primary sources (retrieved 2026-10-28). The GDOL UI tax reporting and liability publication and the GDOL employer FAQs confirm the $9,500 taxable wage base and the 2.70% new-employer rate. The 2026 GDOL rate notice (Form DOL-626: 2026 experience rates and Administrative Assessment rate) has not been retrieved -- the 2026 experience-rated UI rates are not yet confirmed; the wage base and new-employer rate above are confirmed from the archived GDOL sources.

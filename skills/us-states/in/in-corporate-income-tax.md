# Indiana corporate income tax, 2025

> **Source-cited draft.** Built from the Indiana Department of Revenue (DOR) IT-20 Corporate Income Tax Booklet 2025 (SP 259, covering tax year 2025 and fiscal years ending in 2026), the DOR tax types page, and the DOR form pages, read on the dates in Sources. No accountant has reviewed it. Do not file from it without review by a qualified professional.

## Who this applies to

Indiana taxes corporations on adjusted gross income (AGI) under IC 6-3. The tax is administered by DOR (in.gov/dor).

- **C corporations** file Form IT-20 (DOR "Choosing the Proper Income Tax Form" page: "Corporation: The corporation must file a Form IT-20").
- **S corporations** file IT-20S (same page); income is taxed at shareholder level.
- **Partnerships, LLPs and LPs** file IT-65; partners report on IT-40 or IT-40PNR (same page).
- **Nonprofits:** exempt status requires IRS recognition plus an Indiana application (NP-20A); then IT-20NP or NP-20 (same page).
- **Electronic filing:** for taxable years ending after Dec. 31, 2022, a corporation with more than $1,000,000 in federal gross income must file its IT-20 electronically, and must also e-file any amended return (IT-20 booklet, General Information).
- **Affiliated groups** may elect a consolidated return; unitary groups may file a combined return only with DOR permission (see Cross-border points).

## Rates, bands and thresholds

| Item | Rule | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Corporate AGI tax rate | **4.9%** of Indiana taxable AGI (Form IT-20 line 22 x 4.9% = line 23) | IT-20 booklet: "The corporate AGI tax rate is 4.9%"; DOR tax types page: "The rate is 4.9%" |
| Apportionment | single-factor receipts formula (IC 6-3-2-2), Schedule E; interstate transportation uses Schedule E-7 | IT-20 booklet, Deriving Income from Indiana Sources |
| Nonbusiness income | allocated under IC 6-3-2-2(g) through (k), Schedule F | IT-20 booklet |
| Estimated tax trigger | estimated AGI tax liability **exceeding $2,500** for the year | IT-20 booklet, Quarterly Estimated Payments |
| EFT trigger | required quarterly estimated payment **exceeding $5,000** must be paid by electronic funds transfer | IT-20 booklet, Electronic Funds Transfer Requirements |
| Consolidated return ownership | parent owns at least **80%** of each subsidiary's voting stock and at least **80%** of total stock value | IT-20 booklet, Consolidated Reporting |
| E-file threshold | federal gross income over **$1,000,000** | IT-20 booklet, General Information |

## The taxable base

- Start from federal taxable income on federal Form 1120 (or comparable return) and make the Indiana modifications required by IC 6-3-1-3.5(b) (IT-20 booklet, Adjusted Gross Income Tax).
- Internal Revenue Code references mean the IRC as amended and in effect on Jan. 1, 2023 (IT-20 booklet, What's New for 2025).
- Income from sources inside and outside Indiana is apportioned on Schedule E using the single-factor receipts formula; nonbusiness income is allocated on Schedule F.
- **Intangible-expense addback (Schedule IT-20PIC):** a corporation subject to the AGI tax must add to its taxable income any intangible expenses deducted in determining federal taxable income (addback code 140), and must complete Schedule IT-20PIC even if it claims an exception for all expenses (IT-20 booklet, IC 6-3-2-20).
- **Foreign-source dividends:** the deduction is computed on Schedule IT-20FSD (IT-20 booklet).
- **NOLs:** Indiana NOL carryover deductions are computed on Schedule IT-20NOL, with Schedule NOL-MOD where the booklet requires it (IT-20 booklet).
- **Schedule M** (Alternate Adjusted Gross Income Tax Calculation) is in the DOR current corporate form set.

## Deadlines and filing

- **IT-20 due date:** "Unless otherwise specified, state corporate tax returns are due on the 15th day of the 5th month following the close of the corporation's taxable year" (IT-20 booklet; its entity table gives the same date for IT-20). For a calendar-year corporation that is May 15. The DOR tax types summary page still says April 15 for a calendar-year taxpayer; this guide follows the booklet's filing instructions.
- **Extensions:** "Indiana recognizes federal extensions of time to file." DOR accepts federal Form 7004 or the federal electronic extension; returns postmarked within one month after the federal extended date are timely. A corporation with no federal extension can request an Indiana extension through INTIME (intime.dor.in.gov) with a 90% prepayment, or by letter before the due date (IT-20 booklet). An extension of time to file does not extend the time to pay.
- **Estimated tax:** a corporation whose estimated AGI tax liability exceeds $2,500 must pay quarterly, on the 20th day of the 4th, 6th, 9th and 12th months of its tax year (calendar year: April 20, June 20, September 20, December 20). Each installment must equal the lesser of 25% of the year's AGI tax liability or the annualized income installment under IRC section 6655(e). Pay through INTIME, by EFT, or with Form IT-6 (IT-20 booklet).

## Penalties and interest

- **Late payment:** the greater of $5 or 10% of the balance of tax due. It is not imposed if (1) a valid extension exists, (2) at least 90% of the liability was paid by the original due date, and (3) the rest plus interest is paid by the extended due date (IT-20 booklet, Line 44).
- **Late zero-tax return:** $10 per day past due, up to $250 (IT-20 booklet).
- **Underpayment of estimated tax:** a 10% penalty, computed on Schedule IT-2220. No penalty for a period if the payment meets the annualized installment or 25% of the prior year's final tax liability (IT-20 booklet).
- **EFT failure:** 10% of each required electronic payment made by another means (IT-20 booklet).
- **Dishonored payment:** $35 (IT-20 booklet).
- **Interest:** runs from the original due date to the date of payment; DOR sets the rate each October for the next calendar year in Departmental Notice #3 (IT-20 booklet).

## Cross-border points

- **Apportionment:** business income from activity inside and outside Indiana is apportioned by the single-factor receipts formula (IC 6-3-2-2); interstate transportation corporations use Schedule E-7.
- **Consolidated returns (elective):** affiliates under IRC section 1504 may elect to file consolidated if the parent owns at least 80% of each subsidiary's voting stock and 80% of total value; only members with Indiana income or loss are included. The election is made on a timely return, is shown on Schedule 8-D, and binds later years unless DOR grants permission to revoke (request at least 90 days before the return due date) (IT-20 booklet).
- **Unitary combined returns (by petition):** a taxpayer must petition DOR no later than 30 days after the end of the tax year; permission is granted if combined reporting more fairly reflects Indiana income, limited to the water's edge unless otherwise approved. Form IT-20RECAP reconciles federal taxable income, intercompany eliminations and members' tax (IT-20 booklet).
- **Partnership interests:** where a corporate partner and a partnership are unitary, the partner's share of partnership sales enters the single-factor formula (IT-20 booklet).
- **Foreign-source dividends:** Schedule IT-20FSD deduction.
- **Intangibles held by related companies:** Schedule IT-20PIC addback.

## Worked examples

These examples use hypothetical client figures and the confirmed 4.9% rate.

### Worked example 1: flat AGI tax

A C corporation has $2,000,000 of Indiana taxable AGI after apportionment (Form IT-20 line 22).

```oa-check
name: IN corporate AGI tax at 4.9%
inputs:
  agi: 2000000
  rate: 0.049
steps:
  - tax: agi * rate
expected:
  tax: 98000.00
```

Line 23 tax is $98,000. Because it exceeds $2,500, the corporation must also make quarterly estimated payments for the year.

### Worked example 2: intangible-expense addback

The same corporation deducted $500,000 of royalties paid to a related company and has no exception, so Schedule IT-20PIC adds the $500,000 back.

```oa-check
name: IN IT-20PIC addback effect at 4.9%
inputs:
  intangible_expense: 500000
  rate: 0.049
steps:
  - added_tax: intangible_expense * rate
expected:
  added_tax: 24500.00
```

The addback raises Indiana tax by $24,500 (before apportionment, if the $500,000 is apportionable business income).

## Ask the client first

- Is the corporation part of an affiliated group that has elected (or should elect) to file consolidated, and is Schedule 8-D complete?
- Has the group petitioned DOR for unitary combined filing within 30 days after year end?
- Are royalties or interest paid to related companies, and has Schedule IT-20PIC been prepared?
- Did federal gross income exceed $1,000,000 (mandatory e-file)?
- Is a federal extension in place, and was at least 90% of the tax paid by the original due date?
- Were quarterly estimates paid by the 20th day of the 4th, 6th, 9th and 12th months?

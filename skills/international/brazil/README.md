# Brazil: tax pack

Brazil taxes resident companies on worldwide income at the federal
level only: IRPJ (corporate income tax) at 15% plus a 10% surcharge on
annual taxable income over BRL 240,000, computed monthly under lucro
real (actual), presumido (presumed percentages of gross revenue) or
arbitrado, alongside CSLL (9% of the same base, not deductible for
IRPJ). Indirect taxes are the state ICMS and municipal ISS (the CBS/
IBS consumption-tax reform is being phased in — see brazil-vat).
Individuals pay IRRF wage withholding and progressive annual tax; from
2026 there is an exemption band up to BRL 5,000/month and a 10%
withholding on dividends above BRL 50,000/month per payer to resident
individuals (Lei 15.270/2025). Social contributions run through INSS
(employer 20% of payroll plus RAT and "third-party" entities, employee
progressive to 14% capped at the teto), FGTS (8% employer deposit) and
system-s contributions. Transfer pricing follows the OECD-aligned
Lei 14.596/2023 arm's-length regime. Administration is the Receita
Federal do Brasil (RFB, gov.br/receitafederal); currency is the real
(BRL); the tax year is the calendar year.

**Source status:** gov.br/receitafederal and planalto.gov.br statute
texts resolved on 2026-10-24 (Lei 9.249/1995, Lei 7.689/1988,
Lei 8.212/1991 consolidated, Lei 8.036/1990 consolidated,
Lei 9.430/1996, Lei 14.596/2023, Lei 15.270/2025, Lei 13.259/2016 all
fetched and read). The PwC Brazil chapter (reviewed 23 September 2026)
supplies the WHT/treaty table. INSS ceiling/third-party rates for 2026
are carried from the reviewed br-inss skill with its citations.

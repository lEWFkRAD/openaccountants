# Haiti tax pack (HT)

> Source-cited draft for tax year 2026. Last source check: 2026-10-24.

## Coverage

### applicable

- `ht-company-tax-and-advances.md` — business/company income tax.
- `ht-vat-turnover-tax-tca.md` — Taxe sur le Chiffre d'Affaires (TCA); this covers the VAT/turnover-tax domain.
- `ht-payroll-withholding.md` and `ht-payroll-withholding-and-employer-tax.md` — wage withholding and payroll taxes.
- `ht-transfer-pricing.md` — new 2026 Code Général des Impôts arm's-length and documentation rules.
- `ht-cross-border-tax.md` — nonresident services, distributions, interest and withholding controls.
- `ht-social-contributions.md` — ONA/OFATMA scope and evidence controls; current contribution rates are flagged `needs-sources`.

### not_applicable

- Estate/inheritance tax — outside issue #297's missing-domain scope; obtain succession-specific advice before advising on transfers at death.
- Separate exit/departure tax — no generally applicable stand-alone exit-tax regime was established from the official authorities reviewed for this pack; ordinary realization and transfer-pricing rules can still apply.

## Source status

The official DGI PDF of the Code Général des Impôts published in *Le Moniteur*, Special No. 48-A of 30 September 2026, was opened directly and used for the transfer-pricing and withholding guides. Because this is a newly published code, confirm its effective date, transitional provisions, forms and DGI implementation before filing.

ONA and OFATMA official pages confirm the institutions and covered risks but do not publish a complete current 2026 payroll-rate table. Those rates, ceilings and remittance dates are therefore `needs-sources`; the guide does not invent them.

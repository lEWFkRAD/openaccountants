# Argentina: tax pack

Argentina taxes individuals and companies under the Income Tax Law
(Ley 20.628, text ordered, as amended by Ley 27.430), administered by
ARCA (Agencia de Recaudación y Control Aduanero, ex-AFIP). The
corporate rate is tiered 25% / 30% / 35% (top tier above roughly ARS
1.017 billion, indexed); individuals pay progressive fourth-category
Ganancias on employment and personal income. VAT is 21% standard
(10.5% reduced / 27% elevated) under Ley 23.926 with a broad
withholding apparatus. Social contributions run through the SIP
(11% employer / 3% employee up to the monthly tope, plus obra social,
union and ART levies) collected on the monthly F931. The currency is
the Argentine peso (ARS); the fiscal year is normally the calendar
year. Non-residents face a withholding grid (7% dividends, 35% on
deemed margins for interest and royalties) modulated by a 20+ CDI
treaty network. Bienes Personales taxes worldwide assets annually.

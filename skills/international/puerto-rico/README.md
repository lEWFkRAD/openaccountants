# Puerto Rico: tax pack

Puerto Rico levies income tax under its own Code — "Código de Rentas
Internas de Puerto Rico de 2011" (Ley 1-2011, official OGP consolidated
revision of 28 May 2026) — while social security runs through the US
federal system (FICA/FUTA): there is no local social-security contribution
title in the Code. Regular corporations pay a 18.5% normal tax plus a
graduated 5%-19% surtax (Code §§1022.01-1022.02, read 2026-10-27); sales
tax is the IVU at 6% state plus 0.5% municipal (§§4020.01, 4020.10, read).
Withholding is broad: 10% on services (§1062.03, read), 10% on settlements
(§1062.02, read), 30% on partnership and shareholder allocations
(§§1062.04-1062.05, read). Non-resident foreign corporations pay 29% on
fixed determinable PR-source income and 10% on dividends (§1092.01, read).
The currency is the US dollar; the tax year is the calendar year for
individuals and most corporations, with returns due 15 April (§1061.16,
read). The corporate income domain is already covered by
pr-company-income-tax; this pack adds VAT, SC, PR, TP and XB.

## Source status

The OGP consolidated Code PDF (revision 28 May 2026) was read directly;
all § citations in these skills are from that text. Items the Code does
not itself carry — the IVU special-rate schedules from Ley 110-2020 and
later amendments, the US federal FICA/FUTA rates, and the Code's
transfer-pricing section number — are marked needs-sources and must be
confirmed before a tier-1 review.

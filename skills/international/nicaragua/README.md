# Nicaragua: tax pack

Nicaragua runs a reinforced territorial income-tax system under Law 822
(Ley de Concertación Tributaria, 2012, as amended): calendar tax year,
30% corporate rate with a 1%–3% definitive minimum tax on gross, a
progressive labour-income scale to 30%, and a 15% IVA administered by
the Dirección General de Ingresos (DGI). Non-residents are taxed on
Nicaraguan-source income through definitive withholding (20% general,
3% insurance/transport/telecom, 1.5% reinsurance) whether or not they
have a permanent establishment. No income-tax treaties are in force
per the pack overview; an official treaty list did not resolve this
pass and is flagged needs-sources. Currency is the córdoba (C$ / NIO).
The DGI portal is a JS app whose statute PDFs are not directly
linkable; the official Ley 822 primer PDF (dgi.gob.ni/pdfArchivo/22)
was the primary read of 2026-10-23.

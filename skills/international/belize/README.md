# Belize: tax pack

Belize taxes most businesses on **gross receipts** under the Income and
Business Tax Act (Cap. 55) with activity-based rates, plus a general sales
tax (GST), customs and excise duties, real property tax and stamp duty. A
25% net income tax applies only to petroleum operations, which carry the
statute's express arm's-length rules. There is no general transfer-
pricing code — the gross-receipts base limits profit-shifting — but a 3%
withholding on gross contract payments (s.112, regularized by the Income
and Business Tax (Amendment) Act 2024) and double-tax-treaty relief
extended to all treaty countries in 2024 shape the cross-border picture.
Currency BZD is pegged at BZD 2.00 = USD 1.00.

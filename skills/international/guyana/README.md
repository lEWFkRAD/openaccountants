# Guyana: tax pack

Guyana taxes income under the Income Tax Act (Cap 81:01) and companies
under the Corporation Tax Act (Cap 81:03): commercial companies pay 40%
on chargeable profits (2% of turnover minimum corporation tax, whichever
is higher); individuals pay progressive brackets administered by the Guyana
Revenue Authority (GRA). VAT is 14% general (16% on cell-phone services,
with zero-rated and exempt schedules) under the VAT Act 2004 (Act No. 10
of 2004). The currency is the Guyana dollar (GYD); the tax year is the
calendar year. Guyana's income-tax treaty network is narrow and its
transfer-pricing rules sit inside the Income Tax Act's associated-
enterprise provisions rather than a standalone TP code.

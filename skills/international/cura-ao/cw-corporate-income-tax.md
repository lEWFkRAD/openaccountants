# Curacao Corporate Income Tax

## Corporate profit tax (winstbelasting)

- **Profit tax rate on first ANG 500,000** — 15 percent ("Het winstbelastingtarief bedraagt 15% over de eerste 500.000 belastbare winst en 22% over het meerdere (algemeen tarief)")  _([Belastingdienst Curaçao, Winstbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/winstbelasting/))_
- **Profit tax rate above ANG 500,000** — 22 percent (same general-rate sentence on the Belastingdienst winstbelasting page)  _([Belastingdienst Curaçao, Winstbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/winstbelasting/))_
- **Tax base** — Worldwide commercial profit of resident entities, with a participation exemption for qualifying shareholdings; territorial elements apply to certain foreign income  _(Profit Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de winstbelasting 1940))_
- **Reduced 3% regime** — 3 percent (laag belaste activiteiten: ship/aircraft construction, repair and maintenance; supporting services; storage and logistics processing; services to investment funds; not available to notaries, lawyers, public accountants, tax advisers or management)  _([Belastingdienst Curaçao, Winstbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/winstbelasting/))_
- **Exempt company / E-zone reduced rates** — Curaçaose Beleggingsvennootschap 0 percent (since 1 July 2018); doelvermogens 10/T (T = general rate); E-zone companies 2 percent (rate applied through tax year 2022)  _([Belastingdienst Curaçao, Winstbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/winstbelasting/))_
- **Participation exemption** — Qualifying dividends and capital gains from substantial shareholdings are exempt, subject to conditions  _(Profit Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de winstbelasting 1940))_
- **Pillar Two minimum tax (2025)** — 15 percent (Large multinational groups must pay an effective minimum of 15% (OECD Pillar Two top-up))  _([Minimum Tax Ordinance / OECD Pillar Two (GloBE)](https://www.grantthornton.com.cw/insights/TaxLawChanges2025/))_
- **Withholding tax on dividends** — 0 percent (no dividend withholding tax)  _([Profit Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de winstbelasting 1940)](https://hbnlawtax.com/tax-instant-news/end-of-the-year-tax-tips-curacao/))_
- **Withholding tax on interest** — 0 percent (no interest withholding tax)  _([Profit Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de winstbelasting 1940)](https://hbnlawtax.com/tax-instant-news/end-of-the-year-tax-tips-curacao/))_
- **Withholding tax on royalties** — 0 percent (no royalty withholding tax)  _([Profit Tax Ordinance (Landsverordening op de winstbelasting 1940)](https://hbnlawtax.com/tax-instant-news/end-of-the-year-tax-tips-curacao/))_
- **Profit tax return and payment** — Self-assessment (aangiftebelasting) via the online portal; a provisional (voorlopige) return and payment are due within three months after the end of the calendar or fiscal year, and the provisional amount must be at least the tax ultimately owed  _([Belastingdienst Curaçao, Winstbelasting](https://belastingdienst.cw/ondernemer/themas/winstbelasting/))_

Curacao taxes company profits under the Profit Tax Ordinance (winstbelasting). Since 1 January 2023 a two-tier rate applies, and from 2025 a Pillar Two-style minimum tax applies to large multinational groups. Curacao notably levies no withholding tax on outbound dividends, interest, or royalties.

# Colombia: tax pack

Colombia taxes income and consumption under the Estatuto Tributario (ET),
administered by the Dirección de Impuestos y Aduanas Nacionales (DIAN);
enforcement of withholding and social-contribution shortfalls sits with the
Unidad de Gestión Pensional y Parafiscales (UGPP). The tax year is the
calendar year; thresholds and brackets are expressed in UVT (Unidad de Valor
Tributario), an annually indexed unit (COP 49,799 for 2025 per the existing
pack skills). Corporate income tax is 35% with sector surcharges and a 15%
minimum effective tax rate; national VAT is 19%. Colombia operates a broad
withholding-agent system (retención en la fuente) filed monthly on Form 350,
a statutory transfer-pricing regime in ET arts. 260-1 to 260-10 with
regulatory decrees published by DIAN, and a treaty network published on
DIAN's conventions pages.

**Source status:** dian.gov.co (root, TP normatividad page, treaty PDFs) and
normograma.dian.gov.co resolve and were read 2026-10-24; pgn.gov.co (the
gazette mirror for Ley 2277 de 2022) did **not** resolve from this
environment, so statute-level article text for the 2022 reform and the
current annual withholding-resolution numbers are **needs-sources** and
carry the earlier drafts' secondary citations.

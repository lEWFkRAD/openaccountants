# Kentucky (US-KY) pack

Jurisdiction: Kentucky, United States. Tax year 2026. Pack drafted 2026-10-27 (source-cited drafts, tier 2).

Kentucky taxes corporations at a flat 5% with mandatory unitary combined filing (or an
elective same-as-federal consolidated group), pairs the income tax with the Limited
Liability Entity Tax (LLET), and runs unemployment through the Education & Workforce
Development Cabinet. Individuals pay a flat income tax with withholding.

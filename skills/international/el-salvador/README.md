# El Salvador (SV): tax pack

El Salvador taxes on a **territorial** basis (reformed Income Tax Law articles 3, 14-A, 16, 27): only Salvadoran-source income is taxed, in US dollars, on a calendar fiscal year (1 January–31 December). The tax authority is the Ministerio de Hacienda / Dirección General de Impuestos Internos (DGII) at hacienda.gob.sv. The corporate income tax (CIT) is 30% of taxable income, with a reduced 25% rate for taxable income up to USD 150,000, plus a 1.75% monthly advance on gross revenues credited against the annual CIT (F-10/F-11 cycle, annual return by 30 April). Individuals pay the progressive renta table (0/10/20/30%) with the 2025 reform exempt threshold at USD 6,600 (see `el-salvador-income-tax`). Payroll carries ISSS health contributions and AFP pension contributions (see `el-salvador-social-contributions`, `el-salvador-payroll`) plus the INSAFORP 1% training levy for employers with more than ten employees. IVA is 13% (see `el-salvador-iva`). Outbound payments face 20% withholding on Salvadoran-source payments to non-residents (25% for tax-haven/preferential regimes; 5% dividends; 10% interest).

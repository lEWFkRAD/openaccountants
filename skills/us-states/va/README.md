# Virginia (US-VA): tax pack

Virginia levies a graduated individual income tax (2%–5.75%, top rate from just $17,000 of Virginia taxable income) and a flat **6% corporate income tax** on Virginia taxable income -- Va. Code § 58.1-400, statute text verified on law.lis.virginia.gov 2026-10-27. There is **no state-level employee social contribution**: the only state payroll levies are employer-only unemployment insurance (Title 60.2, wage base $8,000 -- among the lowest in the US) and workers' compensation insurance. Sales & use tax (5.3% state rate plus local add-ons) is administered by the Department of Taxation. Virginia has no estate tax and no state exit tax.

**Source status (2026-10-27):** law.lis.virginia.gov statute pages verified 200 with real text for §§ 58.1-15, 58.1-303, 58.1-332, 58.1-400, 58.1-402, 58.1-416, 58.1-422, 58.1-442, 58.1-446, 58.1-455, 58.1-461, 58.1-468, Title 60.2 Ch. 1; tax.virginia.gov root, /corporation-income-tax, /penalties-and-interest, /withholding-tax and vec.virginia.gov all 200 with text. **needs-sources:** §§ 58.1-338.01 / 58.1-338.02 (nonresident & part-year returns) and 58.1-326.1 (PTET) returned no section body from law.lis this run; the Virginia Paid Family Leave premium (program live 1 July 2026, 0.5% of wages split 50/50) could not be verified -- tdi.virginia.gov and paidfamilyleave.virginia.gov both failed DNS 2026-10-27; 2026 SUI wage base/rate schedule (2025: $8,000, 0.10%–6.20% per `va-payroll`).

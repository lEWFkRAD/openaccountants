# Bolivia: tax pack

Bolivia taxes on a territorial **source** principle. Corporate income tax
(IUE, Ley 843) is 25% of Bolivian-source net income; a parallel monthly
transaction tax (IT, 3% of gross revenue) is collected as an advance
against the annual IUE, so a company effectively pays whichever of the
two is higher. Individuals pay RC-IVA (a complementary regime on
own-profession and capital income) and PIT on wages. The consumption tax
is IVA (13% general / 14% financial) on Bolivian-source supplies.
Withholding on payments to non-residents is 12.5% on dividends,
interest, royalties and services (2.5% for partially-performed
activities, 16% for non-domiciled individuals working in Bolivia).
Administration is the Servicio de Impuestos Nacionales (SIN,
impuestos.gob.bo) under the Codigo Tributario (Ley 2492); currency is
the boliviano (BOB); the fiscal year varies by sector and the IUE
return is due 120 days after year-end. There is no transfer-pricing
statute: related-party pricing is policed through the IUE deductibility
rules and the Codigo Tributario general anti-avoidance rule.

**Source status:** impuestos.gob.bo, siat.impuestos.gob.bo and
mintrabajo.gob.bo resolved on 2026-10-24. The official gazette
(gacetaoficial.gob.bo) did not resolve this pass, and the Ley 843 /
Codigo Tributario article numbers for the related-party and
anti-avoidance provisions are **needs-sources** — statutory rates and
deadlines below are cited to the SIN portal and to the PwC Worldwide
Tax Summaries Bolivia chapter (reviewed 21 July 2026).
